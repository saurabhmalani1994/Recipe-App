# Brief S7a: settings, kitchen list, favorites, my recipes and forks (app)
From the orchestrator, release v0.1. Read docs/PRODUCT.md Features items 2, 7, 8, 9 and
"Servings rule", and docs/DECISIONS.md D11 and D15. Read app/README.md and the S2 report at
orch/reports/S2.md.
Base: claude/funny-thompson-jcmpox HEAD. Scope: `app/` only.
Model: mid. Effort: medium.

Deliverable:
1 Settings screen: people (default 2), servings_per_person (1.5), units (metric/US) and the
  diet preset. These are stored in user.db settings through the Db layer. Move the diet switch
  off localStorage onto settings, as open item 1 in the S2 report. Also: the "My kitchen has"
  equipment checklist (the equipment list is in orch/briefs/S5a-diet-equipment.md item 2),
  and pantry staples defaults (salt, pepper, neutral oil, water, plus sugar, flour, butter and
  olive oil, which the user can untick).
2 Kitchen list ("what I have"): add items with type-ahead against a slug list. Use a small
  JSON export for now, `app/src/corpus/slugs.json`, generated by a script from
  ingest/taxonomy/ingredients.yaml (slug, name, synonyms, aisle). Search by any synonym
  (cilantro, coriander or dhania all find it). Items can be removed and are grouped by aisle.
3 Favorites: a star on the recipe detail (the draft fixture recipe view) and a favorites list.
4 My recipes: create and edit your own recipes (title, servings, ingredient lines, steps, notes,
  cuisine, tags). There is also "Make my version" on any recipe, which forks it into My recipes
  with parent_id. The fork's view shows a diff against the parent: ingredients added, removed
  and changed, and steps changed.
5 Recipe detail: scale to people x servings_per_person, rounded up to the recipe's natural unit
  (D11), with a units toggle. Scale the draft fixture's quantities only; the conversion tables
  arrive with the corpus.
6 Tests: unit tests for the settings round-trip, synonym search, scaling (2 people x 1.5 = 3),
  the fork diff, and the backup round-trip including the new data. Add one Playwright walk:
  set diet, add a kitchen item via a synonym, favorite, fork with an edit, see the diff, and
  reload to check it persists. Save screenshots of each screen at 412x915 to app/e2e/screens/.
Out of scope: the matching engine, planner and grocery list, and corpus.db.
Checks: scripts/check.sh summary, plus the e2e count. Report per §6.
