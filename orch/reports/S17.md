- Bug found and fixed during the smoke test: `nyonyacooking.com` emits `recipeYield` as a bare
  JSON integer (not a string) on some pages, which crashed `clean_text()` with a `TypeError`
  on every page (`written: 0` on the first attempt, all 20 pages double-counted under both
  "parse error" and "no schema" — a second bug in the same commit, the crawl loop was
  double-dropping after a parse exception). Both fixed: `_first()` coerces to `str`, and the
  crawl loop no longer also counts "no Recipe LD" after a parse-error drop. Covered by
  `test_fetch_sites_non_string_yield_coerced`. Re-ran clean afterward (20/20, 0 drops).

- Design was wrong about: the brief's examples for Singaporean (noobcook.com) and Chinese
  (redhousespice.com) don't survive contact — noobcook.com has no schema.org Recipe markup at
  all (plain HTML, no recipe-card plugin) and redhousespice.com WAF-blocks every plain-GET
  page request (403, `curl` and `requests` alike, with a normal browser User-Agent). Also,
  several sites (vegrecipesofindia.com, maangchi.com, mykoreankitchen.com, justonecookbook.com,
  rasamalaysia.com, themediterraneandish.com, amiraspantry.com, unicornsinthekitchen.com,
  familyspice.com — all well-regarded, exactly the kind of site the brief expected to work)
  are unreachable by a plain HTTP client (403 WAF-block or a permanent bot-challenge status),
  so "generic sitemap + JSON-LD fetcher" quietly excludes a chunk of the biggest names in this
  space; a headless-browser fetcher (as S1 used for allrecipes.com) is the only way to reach
  them, and is out of this slice's scope.
