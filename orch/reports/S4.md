Other notes:
- Units: an entry without `per` uses `x`, a multiple of the target quantity. With `per: <unit>`,
  amounts are absolute per one unit of target (for example per: egg, or per: cup). The validator
  enforces that absolute units need `per`.
- Quality spread: 3 = 110, 2 = 483, 1 = 197. Where a ratio is uncertain the note says so and the
  quality is lowered, for example agar for gelatin (q1), kosher vs table salt (q2, brand
  density), and vanilla bean per tsp extract.
- Flags are conservative where brands differ, and the note says so: bbq_sauce is flagged fish
  (Worcestershire in many brands), soy sauce, gochujang and hoisin gluten, asafoetida gluten
  (wheat compounding), vegan cheeses nuts (cashew base), and peanuts under nuts.
- Authoring: I wrote the YAML from a compact scratch source through a one-off generator that is
  not committed. The YAML files are now the source of truth. Edit them directly, then run
  `python3 validate.py --coverage` and pytest.
