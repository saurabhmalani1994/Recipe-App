- Design was wrong about: the brief's grocery example ("1 can plus 400 g of tomatoes") reads as
  if it should render as counts ("2 x 400 g cans"); I judged that mixing a plain 400 g line with
  a canned one can't stay in package notation (the plain line isn't a can), so it converts both
  to grams and sums (test 3 in `aggregate.test.ts`). Package notation ("2 x 400 g cans") only
  fires when *every* line for a slug is the same container — covered by a case in
  `groceryRepo.test.ts`'s build, not by a named unit-test case since the brief didn't ask for one.

  "Add to plan" button on Cook results and recipe detail instead, which the brief's three named
  entry points cover without needing favorites to carry corpus keys.
