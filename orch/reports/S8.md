- Changed:
  - `ingest/fetch/fetch_foodcom_ratings.py` (new): the Food.com ratings. The only mirror I could
    reach without Kaggle auth is Hugging Face `peterpeeterspeter/recipe-interactions`. It is
    `interactions_train` from the Food.com Recipes and Interactions set: 698,901 reviews on 160,901
    recipes, keyed by Food.com's own recipe id. It writes `raw/foodcom_ratings/ratings.tsv`. A
    rating of 0 counts as a review with no stars. I found no mirror of RAW_recipes or of the full
    1.1M RAW_interactions.
  - `ingest/curate/` (new):
    - `features.py`: the per-recipe features and the junk detectors.
    - `scan.py`: sharded, resumable, 4 processes, run under a time budget. It runs the build's own
      `curate.drop_reason` and `build_corpus.derive` on every raw line.
    - `dedupe.py`: groups recipes whose normalized titles match and whose slug sets have Jaccard
      >= 0.8, keeping the best as the leader.
    - `score.py`: the documented 0-1 score. Weights are domain 0.20, parse 0.15, quantities 0.10,
      steps 0.15, lines 0.05, time 0.05, Bayesian rating 0.15, popularity 0.10 and extras 0.05.
      A missing core ingredient halves the score. The five domain tiers were set by editorial
      process, and the docstring says how.
    - `select.py`: the selection. It keeps the editorial sources, gives each cuisine a floor of
      min(available, 1500), then fills by score with guards for the mix.
    - `rank.py`: writes `selection.tsv`, `drops.json`, `pool.tsv.gz`, `stats.json` and
      `CURATE_REPORT.md`.
    - `eval/draw.py`: draws `owner_grade.md` (60 recipes, blind) and its key, and scores the
      grades once they are filled in.
  - `ingest/build`: `build_corpus --select SELECTION_TSV` builds exactly the selected lines to
    `/home/user/recipe-data/out/corpus.db`. It stores the curate score as `quality`, stores a
    joined rating when the source has none, and merges `drops.json` into `build_drops` once.
    `derive()` is pulled out so the scan tags exactly what the build writes, and the build loop
    now logs progress every 5,000 lines. `sample.select_lines` and `load_selection` are new.
    `curate.DROP_REASONS` gains `selection_mismatch` and six `curate_*` reasons. There is a new
    generated `ingest/build/CURATED_BUILD.md`.

- Design was wrong about:
  - "RAW_recipes and RAW_interactions: 230k recipes, 1.1M reviews" on a public mirror. Only the
    698,901-review train split is reachable. RAW_recipes is not needed, since the join is by the
    id in the URL.
  - A junk detector for "missing core ingredient". I read 40 flagged recipes by hand and about
    15 were real misses ("Egg Drop Soup" with no egg, "Baked glazed ham" with no ham). The rest
    were dish words the taxonomy also knows as ingredients. So it halves the score and does not
    drop.
  - The parse and time signals barely vary. 97-99% of lines resolve on every site, and 96% of
    times are estimated from the steps. Domain, steps and popularity do most of the ranking.
  - "Every cuisine with data gets min(available, 1500)". The cuisine labels come from the S5b
    classifier. 33.8% of the corpus has no cuisine, and 43% of the slots go to floors. A floor
    also takes low scorers when little is available (east_west_african keeps all 21).
  - The editorial sources are not fixed. bbcgoodfood grew from 2,433 to 3,755 lines between my
    scan and the build. The 1,322 new lines are not in this selection; a rescan picks them up.
  - The foodcom source holds only 1,228 recipes: the upstream csv has 1,048,543 rows, of which
    1,228 parse.
