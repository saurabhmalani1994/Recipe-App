    and ancestors. The validator refuses unknown category/aisle/flag, explicit_meat without an
    animal flag, animal_derived without hidden_animal, a bad parent or a parent cycle, and one
    name for two slugs. Case, accents, punctuation, plurals and ~150 misspellings are normalized
    the same way on both sides of a lookup.

    {qty, qty_max, unit, slug, raw_name, prep, optional, note, pkg}. It handles fractions,
    unicode fractions, "1-1/2", a lost slash ("1 12 teaspoons", "14 teaspoon"), ranges, "1 (14
    oz) can" / "can (28-oz)" / "2 x 400g cans" (the size goes to `pkg`), "1 cup plus 2 tbsp"
    (summed), metric/US/CJK units, parentheticals, "X or Y", "juice of 1 lime", postfix units
    ("4 garlic cloves"), headers, and "salt and pepper" / "1 egg plus 2 egg whites" / "2 each
    carrots, leeks" (several items). `pkg` is one field beyond the brief's list; grocery totals
    need the can size.

    line plus 69 hard lines picked on purpose. `score.py` scores exact matches per field; the
    bar was written in it before the first run. `coverage.py` + `COVERAGE.md` give coverage,
    drop counts and the top 50 unresolved names with reasons.
