  - **Bug #1** (S11+S12 merge, `routes/Cook.tsx`): a "Mine" result's star called

- Open:
  - `inferFromSteps.ts`'s equipment/timing regexes are a simplification, not a port of
    `equipment.py`'s oven-vs-stovetop/grill-vs-broiler disambiguation or `timing.py`'s
    active/passive split and source-field precedence — a suggestion the owner reviews, so a
    false positive (e.g. "toaster" catching a plain toaster oven aside) costs at most one
    ignored suggestion, but it is not the corpus tagger's accuracy.
  - The "N of your recipes hidden" note lists every distinct reason across all hidden My
    Recipes combined (e.g. "no time set, no equipment set"), not a per-recipe breakdown; fine
    for the brief's example phrasing, but the owner may want to see which reason applies to
    which recipe if several filters are active with different recipes missing different fields.
  - A My Recipe's `equipment` field has no "alternatives" concept (`recipe_equipment_alternatives`
    in the corpus, "a blender or food processor" tagged as either): "use only" against a My
    Recipe checks a plain subset of the equipment list typed/accepted in the editor, so an
    either/or note in the steps has to be resolved by the owner picking one when they fill it in.
