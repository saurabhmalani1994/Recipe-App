- Design was wrong about:
  - The brief said "checkout with `lfs: true`" and also "cache the LFS object". With
    `lfs: true`, the object downloads before any cache can be restored, so the cache would
    save no bandwidth. I check out with `lfs: false`, restore the cache keyed by the oid, and
    then run `git lfs pull`.
  - Rebuilding with `--refresh-grown` alone would not have applied R20 to the 2.2M records
    scanned earlier. The rank's `retitle` covers them without a full rescan.
