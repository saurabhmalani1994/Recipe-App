  a straightforward large dataset with ratings. The HF mirror that has the ratings columns
  (`AkashPS11/recipes_data_food.com`) is itself defective: only 1,228 of its 1,048,543 rows are
  populated, the rest are blank placeholder rows — confirmed two ways (raw CSV parse, and HF's
  own server-side Parquet conversion of the same file both show only 1,228 non-null names). A
  fuller alternative (`Karo8870/food.com-parsed-dataset`, ~1M real rows, rich structured
  ingredients) exists but has no rating columns at all and a `steps` column bug (holds the
  images list instead of instructions), so it wasn't substituted here — noted in
  `ingest/sources.md` as a candidate if more Food.com volume matters later. Everything else in
  the brief held: GitHub git-clone/raw.githubusercontent worked despite github.com HTML/API
  403; allrecipes.com is blocked both via curl (402) and headless Chromium (403, "Simple Page"
  challenge, confirmed with Playwright + `--ignore-certificate-errors` for this container's
  proxy); foodwishes.blogspot.com turned out to have zero schema.org/JSON-LD or microdata
  anywhere (not stated either way in the brief) — it needed a plain-text "Ingredients for N:"
  parser instead, and only works for its ~2010–2019 posts (earlier posts are video-only,
  post-Nov-2019 posts link out to allrecipes.com for the full recipe).
- Open: bbcgoodfood stopped at 245/300 (not gomp/cookdiary related — just rate-limit time
  budget: 1 req/s with a 20s timeout is slow to run interactively). It is fully resumable
