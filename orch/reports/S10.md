- Design was wrong about:
  - Size, not count, limits the corpus. S8b's recipes carry long methods: about 3.5 KB each,
    against 2.35 KB in the S8 build. At that size, 80k recipes cannot fit under 235 MB.
  - "Keep everything that passes the junk filters" would have kept 393 Hindi-language pages
    and 370 French-language pages (myparisiankitchen is mostly French), so I added the
    unparsed filter.
  - A fixture that balances in site and Food Wishes video recipes cannot keep check.sh green.
    engine.test.ts, rows.test.ts and myRecipeMatch.test.ts pin counts over the whole fixture
    (58 candidates, "vietnamese has 5", one pot 30, and so on). Swapping in 110 rows broke 14
    tests. The fixture is therefore the old 300 rows, migrated to schema 3 (`--keep-all`), with
    the old static tables.
    - The 59 Food Wishes rows keep their pre-S1c ids, which no longer exist in raw, so none of
      them has a video_url.
    - myRecipeMatch's "Beef stew excluded under no_red_meat" passes only with the old
      substitutions table. With today's table, beef has a diet-safe substitute.
  - The scan's `veg` tag is stale on the S8-era recipenlg shards. The selection counts 21,303
    "no"; the build's tagger writes 4,992. The build is authoritative, and the mix guard is met
    either way.
