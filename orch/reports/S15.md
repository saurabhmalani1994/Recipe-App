# S15 report: estimate servings where the source gives none (ingest)

Builder `ingest`, slice S15. Hash 0a85e25 on `worktree-agent-a9e6414a1eaa6cf90`, nothing pushed.

## Changed
- `ingest/build/servings.py` (new): the estimator. `estimate(raw, items, course, ing, nutrients)`
  returns `servings`, `servings_source` and a counted yield (`yield_count`, "24 cookies"). The
  tiers go in the brief's order. (a) `source`: a `servings` field, or a head-count yield string.
  (b) `text`: an explicit head count ("serves 4", "divide between 4 plates"), a counted yield
  ("makes 24 cookies", "fill 12 muffin cups", "cut into 16 squares", or a source yield "Makes 16"
  / "84 cookies" / "1 loaf"), a volume yield, a pan ("9x13", "20cm square tin", "loaf tin"), or
  portion items in a main ("4 salmon fillets"). (c) `energy`: S14's total kcal over the course's
  kcal per serving. (d) `mass`: total grams over the course's grams per serving. Weak signals are
  soft-thresholded around a calibrated course prior. Every constant and why is in the module
  docstring.
- `ingest/build/servings_eval.py` (new): the eval (the bar was written in its docstring before
  the first run), plus `--fit`, which prints every calibration table, and `--wide`.
- `ingest/build/build_corpus.py`: the call site. `Writer.write` fills `servings`,
  `servings_source` and, only when the source gave no yield string, `yield_text` from
  `yield_count`. Nutrition now fills per estimated serving. `quality` still scores the source's
  own servings, so rankings are unchanged. `corpus_meta` counts `servings.<tier>`, and the build
  report prints the fill rates. I also fixed a counting bug: the S14 nutrition counters were
  incremented before the INSERT, so a duplicate-key recipe was counted too.
- `schema/corpus.sql`: `servings_source TEXT CHECK (IN ('source','text','energy','mass'))`,
  schema_version and user_version 1 -> 2, and updated `servings`/`yield_text` docs.
  `app/src/corpus/types.ts` was regenerated with gen_types.py.
- `ingest/build/make_fixture.py` has a new `--keys-from DB [--keep-rows]` option.
  `app/src/corpus/fixture.db` was migrated to v2 with every old row kept. Only `servings`,
  `servings_source`, `yield_text` and the 7 nutrition columns come from a rebuild of the same 300
  keys (see "Outside my package").
- App: `CorpusRecipeDetail.tsx` shows "Serves about N (estimated)" when `servings_source` is not
  'source', and scaling still uses the estimate (D11). `corpusRecipe.ts` selects the column. One
  unit test and one e2e walk were updated, since Pad Thai now scales 3/5 (see Checks).

## Design was wrong about
- **The energy and mass tiers (c, d) add nothing over a per-course constant on this bar.** On the
  fit half, total kcal / course kcal hits 119/269 used as is, against 176 for the course prior.
  Mass is 181/582 against 357. Stated servings barely track recipe size: median main kcal is
  1,517 at "serves 2", 2,055 at 4 and 3,103 at 6, and the per-serving ratio's IQR is about 2.5x.
  I kept both tiers as a band around the prior, not a pure constant, so a party-sized recipe
  still scales up and per-serving nutrition is never just the course constant. That costs about
  1 point against the prior.
- **70% is not reachable with this data under this metric.** The best constant per course
  already takes 63%. Desserts state anything from 4 to 16 ("serves 8-12" for a cake), and no
  signal in the recipe text separates them. Text evidence helps where it exists: head counts hit
  24/30 against 18 for the prior, portion items 38/46 against 31. But it exists for only about
  20% of recipes.
- The brief put foodcom outside the corpus, but corpus.db holds 210 foodcom recipes (140 with
  servings). Their ingredient lines often carry no quantity ("sugar"), so only the mass tier
  (60 g per unweighed line) can size them.
