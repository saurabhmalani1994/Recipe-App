- Changed:
  - corpus.db opens read-only on both platforms (`src/db/corpusDb.ts`). Its location is one
    config value, `CORPUS_DB_URL` in `src/corpus/config.ts` (VITE_CORPUS_DB_URL at build time).
    A relative path means bundled: a vite plugin copies fixture.db, or CORPUS_DB_FILE, to
    `assets/databases/corpus.db`. An absolute URL means download on first run. On web, the worker
    fetches it into OPFS once and later visits open that copy; without OPFS it goes into memory.
    On native it uses `copyFromAssets` or `getFromHTTPRequest`, then a readonly CapacitorDb.
    Every path sets `PRAGMA query_only` and refuses a schema_version other than
    CORPUS_SCHEMA_VERSION. The status store reports loading, downloading, ready, missing (404)
    or error, and the screens show it.
  - The OPFS worker now tags each message with a db id (S7b open item 1) and keeps one connection
    per name. corpus.db has its own pool and worker (`recipe-app-corpus`), and user.db keeps
    `recipe-app-opfs`.
  - The engine (`src/features/cook/{engine,swaps,taxonomy}.ts`) is pure TS over the Db.
    Candidates come from SQL shaped like match.sql, with have = kitchen + edits + every
    is_staple slug + all ancestors + direct children. That SQL applies diet (ok/adaptable), R9
    one-pot (one_pot AND course = main), cuisine, max minutes, "my kitchen has" (with either/or
    groups; empty means not set) and "use only" (a subset, and at least one used).
    Substitutable means quality >= 2, every component on hand, a context from course and title,
    and no flag the diet forbids (vegetarian: explicit_meat, R7; no red meat: red_meat, R8).
    Ranking: coverage, then fewest truly missing, then quality, then id. Unreadable lines count
    as missing, as in match.sql.
  - Cook screen (`routes/Cook.tsx`): chips prefilled from the kitchen and editable for this
    search only, with search by synonym. Also a cuisine picker (Any + 26), chips for One pot /
    Under 30 min / Equipment ("use only…" from the kitchen's equipment), and results like
    "missing 1: fish sauce, swap: soy sauce + nori" that link to the detail.
  - Corpus recipe detail (`routes/CorpusRecipeDetail.tsx`, reached from `/recipe/:id` by
    `recipes.key`; the r01-r20 fixture recipes still render as before). It shows ingredients
    scaled by D11, a Metric/US toggle, and a "missing" tag and best swap on each line (on hand
    first, no swaps for staples). It also shows the diet status and its swaps, steps, equipment
    chips, times and the source link. A yield that is not a head count is shown and not scaled.
  - Units (`src/features/units/units.ts`) come from the corpus `units` table and density
    (a parent's density when a variety has none). Metric turns mass into g/kg. Cups, fl oz,
    pints, quarts and gallons become g when the density is known, else ml/l. tsp and tbsp stay.
    US turns g into oz/lb and ml into cups/tbsp/tsp. Package sizes are converted but not
    scaled. `tsconfig.test.json` is new so that tests (and only tests) get node types.

- Design was wrong about:
  - The example "swap: soy sauce + lime": in the curated table, fish_sauce__soy_sauce-lime_juice
    is quality 1, so under the brief's quality >= 2 rule it never shows. The walk shows soy
    sauce + nori (quality 2) instead.
  - "if the OPFS worker needs a per-db id": the id was needed but was not enough. With two dbs
    in one SAH pool, a first visit that opened corpus.db and user.db together failed with
    "SQLITE_NOTADB: file is not a database" (2 e2e failures). Two causes: concurrent pool
    installs, and importDb's streaming form, which claims its pool slot only when it finishes,
    so user.db got the same slot. Fixes: a shared install promise, a separate pool and worker
    for corpus, and importing the whole array.
  - `recipes.unresolved_count` counts raw lines that have no item row at all. In fixture.db that
    is 123 unresolved lines against 92 null-slug items, across 20 of 300 recipes. So the app
    cannot always show the text of an unreadable line, and shows "an ingredient line that could
    not be read" instead.
  - The app's "My kitchen has" list (`data/equipment.ts`, 22 items) is missing 9 corpus
    equipment values: toaster, toaster_oven, sous_vide, waffle_iron, bread_machine, dehydrator,
    campfire, steamer, ice_cream_maker. Once a kitchen is set, recipes needing any of these are
    always filtered out, and the owner cannot tick them. Not changed here: Settings is out of
    scope.
- Open: ranking coverage first (as briefed) puts tiny recipes at the top. In K1 a 2/4 margarita
  is second, ahead of 3/8 salads, and in K2 a 1/1 casserole ties the 10/10 curry at 100%.
  Recommendation: before the §9 bar (8 of the top 10 cooked), have the owner grade one planted
  top 10 under the current rule, then decide whether to require at least 3 needed ingredients or
  to rank by covered count within a coverage band.
